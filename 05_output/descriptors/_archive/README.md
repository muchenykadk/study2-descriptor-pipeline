# Archived highlight images

Feature-highlight PNGs for taxonomy entries that were retired after the run that
produced them (2026-08-27). They show `biological_growth` and `paste_dominant`,
both confirmed absent from this batch by inspection and retired on that basis.

They are kept rather than deleted because they are evidence of what the classifier
returned before the vocabulary was narrowed, which is part of the record of how the
taxonomy was arrived at. Nothing reads them: the reports and the inventory render
only active features.

Regenerating the reports will not recreate them.
